5. [(20 points)] (Minimax Two-Player Coin Sequence Game) An even num-
   ber n of coins with positive integer values ⟨v1 , v2 , . . . , vn ⟩ are arranged in
   a straight line. Two players take turns. In each turn, a player may pick
   either the leftmost or the rightmost available coin from the line. Both
   players play optimally to maximize their own total collected value. More
   precisely, they will adopt maximin optimality: A player will always make
   a move that maximizes the minimum value they would obtain in any
   subsequent scenario.


   Example:       Consider the sequence of coins A = [4, 5, 1, 3].

      • Suppose Player 1 picks 4. Then if Player 2 picks 5, Player 1 would
        pick 3, and the total value Player 1 obtains is 4 + 3 = 7. If Player 2
        picks 3, Player 1 would pick 5, and the total value Player 1 obtains
        is 4 + 5 = 9. So this move guarantees a minimum total value of 7.
      • Suppose Player 1 picks 3. Then if Player 2 picks 4, Player 1 would
        pick 5, and the total value Player 1 obtains is 3 + 5 = 8. If Player 2
        picks 1, Player 1 would pick 5, and the total value Player 1 obtains
        is 3 + 5 = 8. So this move guarantees a minimum total value of 8.
