# VoIP — Voice over Internet Protocol

**What it is.** Carrying telephone calls over packet networks.

**Why it appears here.** Voice is the canonical **real-time** workload: a late
packet is worthless. So jitter and delay hurt far more than occasional loss, and
queueing delay — the price of packet switching — is exactly the wrong enemy.
