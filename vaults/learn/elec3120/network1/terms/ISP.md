# ISP — Internet service provider

**What it is.** The organisation you buy Internet access from.

**Why it appears in the mobility lecture.** Cellular networks have a strong notion
of a **home** network (a paid subscription, roaming agreements, a global identity
on a SIM). ISPs and Wi-Fi do **not**: credentials are just a username/password,
different per network, with no global registry — so there is no "home" to forward
your traffic to. That asymmetry is why mobile IP exists for cellular but is
essentially undeployed for Wi-Fi (with exceptions such as eduroam).
