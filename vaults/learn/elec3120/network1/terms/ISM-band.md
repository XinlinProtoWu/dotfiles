# ISM band — Industrial, Scientific and Medical

**What it is.** Bands of radio spectrum set aside for unlicensed use — no
operator licence required, which is why Wi-Fi and Bluetooth live there.

**Where.** 2.4–2.5 GHz is the crowded one: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and microwave ovens
all share it. That is why Bluetooth hops frequencies (79 channels, pseudo-random
per slot) and why 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi is slow in apartment blocks.
